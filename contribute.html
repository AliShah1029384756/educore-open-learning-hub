Open Source Contribution

Help students by submitting verified free resources.

Har contribution se kisi student ka waqt bachta hai. Sirf high-quality, free, and relevant educational links add karein.

Submit via Google Form

Rule 1: Free and Accessible

Resource free ho, paywall-heavy na ho, aur region-blocked na ho.

Rule 2: Academic Relevance

School, college, entry test, university, ya skill-track ke liye directly useful ho.

Rule 3: Clear Metadata

Type, language, level, and source mention karna zaroori hai.

Rule 4: No Spam

Self-promotion, irrelevant channels, ya low-quality links reject kiye jayenge.

Submission Format

  • Title: concise and topic-focused
  • Type: Video / Read / Practice
  • Language: Urdu / English / Mixed
  • Level: Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced
  • URL: direct and working
  • Short reason: why this resource is valuable
